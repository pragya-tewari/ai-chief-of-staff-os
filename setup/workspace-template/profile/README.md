# Profile

Who you are and how you work. The assistant reads both files at the start of
every session.

- `about-me.md` — your role, who you work with, how you write, your priorities.
  Copied during install from the repo's `rules/about-me.template.md`; complete it
  by asking your assistant to "set up my profile".
- `overrides.md` — your learned changes to the default rules. Grows as you
  correct the assistant. It never touches the shipped product rules; it sits on
  top of them.
