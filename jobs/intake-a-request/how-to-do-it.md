# How to do it

Read the rules first. This is demand management — deciding what to take on, and
saying no well when the answer is no.

## Steps

1. **Capture the request** clearly: who's asking, what they want, why, by when.
2. **Decide: do / defer / decline / route.**
   - *Do* — it fits the goals and there's capacity: turn it into a task.
   - *Defer* — it's real but not now: park it with a revisit date.
   - *Decline* — it doesn't fit, or the cost outweighs it: write the no.
   - *Route* — it belongs to someone else: send it there.
3. **Check it against the goals** (`org/goals.md`). A request that serves no goal
   is a candidate for defer or decline, not automatic yes.
4. **Write the no well.** A good no gives the reason, respects the asker, and
   offers the nearest thing you *can* do. Draft it; sending it needs a yes.
5. **Route the outcome:** a *do* becomes a task; a *defer* a parked item; a
   *decline* a drafted message; a *route* goes to the right owner (with a yes).
