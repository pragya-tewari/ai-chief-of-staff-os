# Example

Mode: `founder`. The weekly update to Dana, using the sample company.

## Input

`run write-an-update in founder`

## Output — a draft in `reports/founder-update-2026-08-24.md`

**Weekly update — 24 Aug**

*Moved*
- Full rollout re-timed to October and the reason is now clean (executive showcase
  vs full rollout were conflated). Vendor has the runway to close the content gap.

*Stuck*
- Vendor content scope was due 26 Aug, not started as of today. Chasing in
  tomorrow's 1:1 with Ravi. If it slips past the week, October is at risk.

*Needs a decision from you*
- Second production vendor or single-source (Q7). Ravi has a view; you hold the
  call. I'll bring both sides to the next strategy slot.

*Asks*
- None this week.

## What needs a yes

Sending this to Dana. Until then it's a draft.

## Notice what it doesn't do

It doesn't reassure, pad, or bury the stuck item. The founder mode is honest and
tight — the same vendor risk the team sees, framed for someone who can act on it.
