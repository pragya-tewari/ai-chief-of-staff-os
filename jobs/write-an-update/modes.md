# Modes

One set of facts, five audiences. This table is the heart of the job.

| Mode | Needs | Never needs | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| `team` | What moved, what's next, what's blocked and who can unblock it | Board-level politics, financials they can't act on | Concrete, direct, close to the work |
| `leads` | Cross-project risk, dependencies, decisions coming | Task-level detail | Coordinating, one level up |
| `founder` | Progress against strategy, the real risks, decisions needed from them, clear asks | Reassurance, padding | Honest, tight, decision-focused |
| `board` | Progress against goals, material risks, decisions the board must make | Operational detail, internal people issues | Formal, numbers checked, measured |
| `company` | The narrative, the wins, what's coming | Anything sensitive or half-settled | Warm, plain, motivating |

The method never changes. Only length, framing, and what's omitted.
