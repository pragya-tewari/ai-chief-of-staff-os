# When to run

After any meeting worth capturing — a status call, a 1:1, a founder or strategy
call, a vendor escalation. Not every quick sync needs it; run it when the meeting
produced decisions, commitments, or context you'll want later.

Triggered by:
- The user saying "run meeting-to-tasks on <transcript>".
- A transcript landing in the inbox from a meeting the cadence expects (see the
  workspace's `org/cadence.md`).

Its partner job, `prepare-a-meeting`, runs *before* a meeting. This one runs
after. Together they cover most of a chief of staff's week.
