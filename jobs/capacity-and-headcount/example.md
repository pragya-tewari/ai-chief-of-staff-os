# Example

Using the sample company.

## Input

`run capacity-and-headcount against intake/headcount.md`

## Output — `reports/capacity-2026-08.md`

**Capacity — August** (from intake/headcount.md)

*Who we have*
- Sam (chief of staff), Ravi (content), Meera (onboarding), Omar (client), plus a
  production vendor contractor rolling off 30 Aug.

*What we're committed to*
- October rollout, onboarding-time goal, renewal. Plus the compliance modules the
  contractor was carrying.

*The gap*
- The contractor leaves with the compliance modules unfinished. Ravi can absorb
  it short-term but then the reporting pack slips — he can't do both in the
  window.

*Options*
- Extend the contractor two weeks (~$4,000) to finish the modules. Cheapest,
  closes the gap.
- Move the rollout comms (T22) to Arun, who has spare capacity, freeing Sam and
  keeping Ravi on content. No cost.
- Do neither and accept the rollout slips into November.

## What needs a yes

Nothing — the report is yours. The choice between options is your call.
