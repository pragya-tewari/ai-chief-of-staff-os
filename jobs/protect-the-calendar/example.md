# Example

Using the sample company. Input is `intake/calendar-august.md` (a time log by
meeting type, plus the recurring meetings).

## Input

`run protect-the-calendar`

## Output — `reports/calendar-2026-08.md`

**Where the time went — August** (from intake/calendar-august.md)

| Kind | Hours | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Status meetings | 22 | 40% |
| 1:1s | 10 | 18% |
| External (client / partners) | 8 | 15% |
| Deep work | 6 | 11% |
| Ad hoc | 9 | 16% |

*Against stated priorities*
- The quarter's priority is the October rollout. Only ~11% of time was protected
  for the deep work it needs; 40% went to status meetings.

*Meetings that should stop*
- The Tuesday and Thursday status calls cover the same ground. Merge to one.
- The weekly "sync" with no owner and no output — drop it; the sweep already
  surfaces what it was for.

*Suggestion*
- Removing one duplicate 30-minute status call and the ownerless 30-minute sync
  recovers ~1 hour a week (roughly 4–5 hours a month) for rollout work.

## What needs a yes

Nothing changed. Merging or dropping a meeting is your call.
