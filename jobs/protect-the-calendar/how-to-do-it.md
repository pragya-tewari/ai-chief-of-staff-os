# How to do it

Read the rules first. Reading a calendar can expose a lot — scope to time
blocks and titles, not the contents of every invite.

## Run identity

Use the calendar/account scope plus the analysed date range. A rerun regenerates
the same report for that range after reconciling the existing receipt and output.

## Steps

1. **Where the time went.** From the calendar, group last month's hours by kind
   of meeting (1:1s, reviews, external, deep work, ad hoc).
2. **Against stated priorities.** From `org/goals.md` — what the leader said
   mattered this quarter.
3. **Find the mismatch.** Where the hours and the priorities disagree.
4. **Which meetings should stop.** Recurring meetings with no clear output, or
   that duplicate another.
5. **Write the report to `reports/`.** Suggest, don't reschedule — moving or
   cancelling a meeting is the leader's action.
