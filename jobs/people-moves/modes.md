# Modes

| Mode | For | The focus |
|---|---|---|
| `joining` | someone new | Access to grant, context to give, first-thirty-days, who introduces them to what |
| `leaving` | someone going | Knowledge to capture before they go, access to remove, their open work redistributed |
| `changing role` | someone shifting | What they pick up, what they hand off, updated ownership |

Same method — access, context, open work — pointed forward, backward, or sideways.
