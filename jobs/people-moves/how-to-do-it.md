# How to do it

Read the rules first. Pick the mode. People moves touch access and open work, so
handle them carefully.

## The shared method

1. **Name the person and the move.** Update or create their file in `people/`.
2. **Access.** What they need (joining) or what to remove (leaving) — as a
   checklist, not an action. Granting or removing access is the user's own doing.
3. **Context.** What they need to know (joining), or what only they know that
   must be captured before they go (leaving).
4. **Open work.** Their tasks from the task source — what to hand over and to
   whom (leaving), or what to assign (joining/role change).
5. **Write the plan to `reports/`** and update the person file. Reassigning tasks
   to real people, or telling anyone, needs a yes.
