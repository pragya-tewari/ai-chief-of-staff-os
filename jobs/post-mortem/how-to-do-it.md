# How to do it

Read the rules first. A post-mortem is about the system, not the person. Keep it
blameless — the goal is a change that sticks, not someone to blame.

## Steps

1. **What happened.** A plain timeline, from the evidence (`meetings/`,
   `projects/`, the task history). Facts, not interpretation.
2. **Why.** The real cause, usually a process or a gap, not a person. Ask "what
   let this happen" not "who did it".
3. **What changes.** The specific change that would stop it recurring — a new
   check, a changed step, a clearer owner.
4. **Who owns the change.** A change with no owner is a wish. Turn it into a task.
5. **Write it up in `reports/`.** If the change amounts to a decision or a new
   rule, that's a decision record (needs a yes) or a proposed override.
