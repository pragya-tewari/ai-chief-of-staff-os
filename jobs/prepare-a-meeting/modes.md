# Modes

Same method every time; the audience and depth change.

| Mode | For | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| `1:1` | a recurring one-on-one | Focus on one person: what they owe, what you owe, what changed for them, what to raise. Short |
| `portfolio review` | the regular review across projects | Focus on project health and cross-project risk; less about any one person |
| `board` | a board meeting | The formal version: progress against goals, risks, decisions the board must make. Tightly framed, numbers checked |
| `all-hands` | a company-wide meeting | The narrative and the hard questions the room will ask; less detail, more framing |
| `external` | a partner, investor, customer or regulator | Who they are, what they want, what you want, and what you will not say |

The method in `how-to-do-it.md` does not change between these. Only the pack's
shape and depth do.
