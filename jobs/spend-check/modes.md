# Modes

| Mode | Looks at | Flags |
|---|---|---|
| `project money` | one project's plan vs actual | Where it's over or under, by how much, and why |
| `recurring spend` | subscriptions and tools | What we pay for, who owns each, what overlaps, what to cut |

Same method — read plan, read actual, find and explain the drift. Only the scope
changes.
