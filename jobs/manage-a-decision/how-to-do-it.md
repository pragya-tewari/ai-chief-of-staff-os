# How to do it

Read the rules first, pick the mode, then follow the shared method. A decision
record is a claim about what was settled, so **writing one always needs a yes.**

## Run identity

The run key is the decision id plus the mode, so framing, aligning, recording and
revisiting the same decision are distinct runs. A repeated `revisit` adds its
review date to the key.

## The shared method

1. **Name the decision** and its `id`. Check `decisions/` — does it already exist
   in some form? Reconcile, don't duplicate.
2. **Who holds the call.** From `org/decision-rights.md`. A decision made by the
   wrong person is not settled.
3. **What's known and what's open.** Separate the settled parts from the open
   ones. Green for settled, amber for open.
4. **Do the mode's specific work** (frame / align / record / revisit).
5. **Route.** A finished record goes to `decisions/` on a yes. Open threads go to
   `questions.md`. A pack for a meeting goes to `reports/`.
