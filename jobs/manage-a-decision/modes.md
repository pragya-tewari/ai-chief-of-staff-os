# Modes

Same decision, four points in its life. The method is one; the work differs.

| Mode | When | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| `before` | a decision is coming up in a meeting | A one-page pre-read: what's settled, what's open, and the named question each person must answer |
| `align` | people hold unreconciled positions | An alignment write-up: who holds what, what each needs to move, what would settle it. Not a one-line question — the detail is what makes it resolvable |
| `record` | a decision just got settled | A decision record: what was decided, by whom, when, why, and what was rejected. Needs a yes |
| `revisit` | months later | A short check: did this work out, and would we decide the same way now? |

The `align` mode is the one that most separates a chief of staff from a project
manager — it holds several people's positions without flattening them.
