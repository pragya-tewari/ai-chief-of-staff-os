# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space this
project uses.

## What we expect

- Clear, direct, respectful communication.
- Feedback aimed at the idea or the code, with a reason attached.
- Patience with people who are new to the project or to the chief-of-staff way
  of working.

## What is not welcome

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning language.
- Sharing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## If something goes wrong

Use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify the
report as a code-of-conduct matter. Do not include sensitive details in a public
issue. Reports are handled privately and in good faith. The maintainer may edit,
remove or reject contributions and comments that break these rules, and may
block repeated offenders.
