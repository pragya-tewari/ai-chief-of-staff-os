# Full rollout moves to October

**Decided:** 16 Aug 2026, founder strategy call (M3).
**By:** Dana (CEO holds rollout timing — org/decision-rights.md).

**What was decided:** the full rollout to all the client's employees moves from
the committed date to October. The executive showcase stays on the committed
date.

**Why:** "the committed date" had been conflated — an executive showcase and a
full rollout on the same date. The vendor's compliance modules can't be done in
time for a full rollout by the committed date. October gives room to close the
gap without a phantom deadline.

**What was rejected:** holding the committed date and rolling out incomplete
content. Rejected because a rollout missing the compliance modules would cost
more trust with the client than the delay.
